# Unbreakable Bonds solo cheat sheet

Solo play is the co-op ruleset minus party skills and envelopes, with each enemy run by its combat board instead of a second player. Source: the official rules PDF; check it for exact wording.

## What changes in solo

**Setup**
- Add the expansion's hero sheets and combat tokens to your supplies.
- Add the Battle Pilgrim, One with the Land and High Society skill sets to the skill supply.
- After building the asset deck, shuffle the 10 twist cards into it.
- Keep the combat boards and their enemy token sets within reach.
- Do not shuffle party skill cards into the skill deck, and do not use the envelope cards.

**During play**
- If you draw an adventure, skill or asset card that refers to another hero, discard it and draw a replacement.
- Skip trading, parties and party skills.
- Read the player-number icon as 1.

**New concepts**
- **Prepare action:** place a prepare token on your hero sheet; on a later turn spend it for 1 extra action. You can hold 1.
- **Twists:** resolve when drawn while shopping, discard, then draw a replacement asset.
- **Enemy traits:** Warrior, Mystic, Savage or Trickster. Warrior is the base game set.

## Running a combat round

1. Note the enemy's trait; pull its tokens (5 in Act I, 6 from Act II) and its combat board.
2. Cast the tokens and place each on the board space for its symbol.
3. Initiative as normal; a tie goes to the hero.
4. On each enemy action, resolve the topmost space that still has tokens, all tokens in it.
5. If a symbol can't be resolved or would do nothing, skip to the next space and check it again next action.
6. When no space can be resolved, the enemy passes for the round.
7. The header ability under the board's title always applies.
8. If you stay in the fight, recast everything and start from the top.

## Edge rules

| Rule | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Surge | Stack surge tokens to trigger the most expensive unused surge ability. If it would do nothing, use another that would. If none would, spend nothing and go to the next space. Equal cost: resolve from the bottom of the card up. Each once per round. |
| Agility abilities | Use the agility text on the enemy card, not surge tokens. |
| Plus (+) | Applies only if tokens of that symbol are being resolved; adds to their combined value. |
| Shield | Spent at the first chance in response to damage. If a shield and another blocking symbol are both available, the shield goes first. |
| Moving tokens | A recast or flipped token moves to its new space; a stack moves to the matching surge section. |
| Enemy choices | Pick what hurts the hero most. If you can't be impartial, deal 2 damage instead. |
| Charge | Spend X charge to deal X unblockable damage; that combatant can't block for the rest of the round. |
| Tactic | Spend one to copy a symbol on any token, or a blocking symbol just before taking damage. Not combined with other tokens. |

## Enemy boards (top to bottom)

Wording is exactly what the CombatBoards_v2 PDF prints. The PDF's header banner is blank, so the header abilities come from the Token Caster. `*` marks the gold (initiative) side; `{2}` means value 2.

| Trait | Header ability | Board order |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warrior | None | Agility, Double, Skull, Surge, Shield |
| Mystic | Any surge left at the end of a combat round deals 1 | Agility, Double, Surge, Skull, Shield |
| Savage | On engage, deal 1 | Double, Surge, Agility, Skull, Shield |
| Trickster | +1 initiative | Agility, Surge, Double, Skull, Shield |

Surge spaces have sections 3, 2 and 1. Skull spaces have no text. Each Shield space has a blank slot beside it.

- **Warrior:** Agility: flip blank to double or skull; otherwise flip foe's highest value token. Double: double surge first; otherwise double highest skull.
- **Mystic:** Agility: flip blank to double (first choice), surge or skull; otherwise flip foe's highest value token. Double: double surge first; otherwise double highest skull.
- **Savage:** Double: double highest skull (first choice) or double surge. Agility: flip blank to double (first choice), surge or skull; otherwise flip foe's highest value token.
- **Trickster:** Agility: flip blank to shield (first choice) or skull; otherwise flip foe's highest value token. Double: double shield first; otherwise double highest skull.

**What "flip blank" means.** A token has two sides. Find one of the enemy's own tokens showing blank whose other side is one of the listed symbols, and turn it over. Use the first choice if available, then the next. If no blank token can become any of them, flip the hero's highest value token instead.

**Where the Token Caster differs.** It adds a Tactic space (Mystic and Trickster) and a Charge space (Savage), bonuses on the Warrior's Skull and Shield, and terser agility text. Some tokens show Tactic or Charge faces that have no space on the PDF boards; the app can switch between the two wordings.

| Trait | Act I tokens | Added in Act II |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warrior | Damage / Agility; Damage / -*; Damage / Shield; Surge / Damage*; Double / -* | Surge* / Damage{2} |
| Mystic | Damage / Agility*; Damage / Surge*; Damage / Double; Surge / Shield; Tactic* / - | Damage{2} / Surge* |
| Savage | Surge / -*; Charge{2} / Surge; Damage / Charge*; Damage / Shield; Damage / -* | Damage{2} / Double |
| Trickster | Surge{2} / Damage; Shield / -*; Damage* / Agility; Tactic / -; Damage / Agility* | Damage{2} / Surge* |

## Scenarios

**The Locust Swarm** (win by defeating all warbands; no separate loss condition in the text I read)
- Shuffle the 5 warbands. Put 1 faceup on story space I and spawn it in Outlaw Glade; add 1 facedown per player to the story track.
- Each turn, from story space I, move each faceup warband 1 hex toward the nearest civilized hex it can target. Scorched earth hexes can't be targeted. With no target, it gains 1 unit instead.
- Targets by units: 1 shrines; 2 plus towns; 3 plus strongholds; 4 plus cities.

**Caught in a Web** (needs that scenario pack)
- At the end of Act I place 1 web in each city. In solo, Queen Ariad spawns immediately.
- Defeat her to win. You lose at the end of Act II or if all heroes are eliminated.
- Entering a hex with or next to a web: the terrain die result must be wild; exert once per hex to ignore.

## Rulings

- Failed a test that makes you discard a trophy and you have none: nothing happens (general convention, not a printed ruling).
